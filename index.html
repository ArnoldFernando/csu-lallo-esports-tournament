Tournament Guidelines

Official Rules

1 Player Requirements

  • Bonafide students of CSU Lal-lo Campus
  • Officially enrolled during the current academic year
  • Must present valid CSU Student ID for verification
  • Must use registered IGN and UID

2 Team Requirements

  • All members from the same program
  • 5 Core Players required
  • 2 Substitute Players (optional)
  • Only registered team members may participate

3 Tournament Format

  • Double-Elimination (Upper & Lower Bracket)
  • 2 losses = elimination
  • Upper Bracket: Best-of-3
  • Lower Bracket: Best-of-3
  • Grand Finals: Best-of-5

4 Time & Match Protocol

  • Be ready 5 minutes before match time
  • 5-minute grace period allowed
  • More than 5 minutes late = automatic DQ
  • Follow official tournament schedule strictly

5 Player Verification

  • Face-to-face, physically present at venue
  • In-person verification before each match
  • Valid CSU Student ID or proof of enrollment required
  • Registered MLBB account must be used
  • Device and account inspection may occur

6 Roster Rules

  • Submit complete roster before deadline
  • Include: Name, IGN, UID, Year, Program, Student ID
  • Deadline: August 21, 2026
  • No changes after deadline without approval
  • Unregistered players = immediate DQ

7 Technical & Game Rules

  • Latest official MLBB version required
  • Teams responsible for own devices & internet
  • 3 pauses per game (type "P" in chat)
  • No pausing during clashes or objective contests
  • Auto-pick is valid and final — no rematch

8 Grounds for Disqualification

  • Deliberate stalling or intentional delay
  • Leaving a match before it ends
  • Use of exploits, hacks, cheats, or unauthorized programs
  • Playing with an alternate or undeclared account
  • Unsportsmanlike conduct, toxicity, harassment
  • Physical violence or threats
  • Betting, gambling, or match-fixing
  • Stream sniping or using live broadcast for unfair advantage
  • Any attempt to compromise tournament integrity
⚠ Tournament officials may conduct additional verification at any time. Falsification of player information will result in disqualification.

Tournament Guidelines

Official Rules

1 General Conduct

  • Professional sportsmanship required at all times
  • No taunting, harassment, or hate speech
  • Cheating, hacks, mods, or exploits = immediate DQ + forfeit
  • Players responsible for device and connection stability
  • Max 5-minute pause for disconnections, no replay beyond that

2 Team Composition

  • Currently enrolled CSU students only
  • 5 main players + up to 1 substitute
  • No players with major COD pro tournament history
  • Roster must be submitted before tournament begins
  • No roster changes once tournament starts
  • Substitutions only between matches (not during a game)

3 Match Procedures

  • Check in with officials 15 minutes before match time
  • Enter lobby within 5 minutes of official match time
  • Failure to enter lobby after 5 min = forfeit
  • Captains must immediately report results to officials
  • Screenshot evidence required for disputes
  • Upload screenshots within 15 minutes after match ends

4 Gun Roles

  • 4 Weapon Class Roles must be declared per player
  • Assault Rifles (ARs) — Assault Rifle class only
  • Sub-Machine Guns (SMGs) — SMG class only
  • Heavy — Shotgun and LMG classes
  • Marksman — Sniper and Marksman Rifle classes
  • Pool: 3 ARs, 3 SMGs, 2 Heavy, 2 Marksman roles

5 Operator Skills

  • Each player declares their Operator Skill before tournament
  • No two players on the same team may use the same skill
  • Each player must have a unique Operator Skill
  • Allowed: Annihilator, Claw, Death Machine, Equalizer, Gravity Spikes, Gravity Vortex Gun, Purifier, Sparrow, Tempest, War Machine

6 Restricted Items

Using any restricted item results in disqualification and the point is awarded to the opponent. New content is restricted for 21 days from release unless stated otherwise.

Restricted Weapons

NA-45, SVD, XPR, Thumper, SKS, Shorty, SMRS, FHJ-18, Argus, VLK Rogue, M1 Grand, MG 82, MG 42, T63, SO14, Melee weapons

Restricted Lethal

Trip Mine, Thermite, Molotov Cocktail, Contact Grenade, C4, Cluster Grenade

Restricted Tactical

Heartbeat Sensor, Gas Grenades, Flash Drone, Echo Grenade, Stim Shot, Cryo Bomb, Storm Ball, Decoy Grenade, Trip Sensor, Douser Grenade

Restricted Perks

Persistence, Restock, Martyrdom, Quick Fix, Hardline, Alert, High Alert, Tracker, Recon, Pinpoint, Unit Support, Tactician, Vulture, Overclock, Engineer, Demo Expert, Survival Training, Assassin

Restricted Attachments

Akimbo, Disable, Thermite/Dragon's Breath/Explosive/Incendiary Ammo (all guns), Slug Ammo (shotguns), and various weapon-specific attachments

Other Restrictions

All Legendary utility skins, all Wildcards, all restricted operator skins, and newly added content within 21 days of release

⚠ All Scorestreaks except Hunter Killer Drone, Predator Missile, and EMP Systems are restricted. All Operator Skills except the 10 listed are restricted.

7 Penalties & Forfeits

  • Minor violations = warnings or temporary penalties
  • Major violations (cheating, toxicity) = immediate DQ
  • Cannot start within 5 min = forfeit Game 1
  • Cannot field at least 4 players = forfeit
  • Game 2 forfeit if 10 minutes late

8 Admin Rights

  • Admin may overrule all rules based on situation
  • Organizer's decision is final after appeal
  • Only coaches or team captains may communicate with Tournament Manager
  • Violation of player conduct = penalties, forfeit, or DQ
Ensure devices are fully charged. No extra time for equipment issues. Take screenshots of every match result.
